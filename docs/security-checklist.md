# Security checklist

Generated by `scripts/security-scan.py`.

## Controls in place

- Passwords: salted scrypt hashes; first passwords must be changed; optional two-step verification (TOTP) for staff.
- Sign-in throttling: account lock for 15 minutes after 8 wrong passwords; student roll number limited to 8 failures / 15 min; any network address limited to 30 failed sign-ins / 15 min (lib/rateLimit.ts).
- Sessions: httpOnly cookies, 7-day expiry, revocable; MFA-pending sessions cannot use staff pages.
- Every API route checks the caller (table below); pages check role and institute; students see only their own record; parents only through a revocable read-only link.
- Results lock on approval; grade changes need a recorded request; every sign-in, approval, export, import, backup and restore is in the audit log.
- Security headers: HSTS, nosniff, Referrer-Policy, X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN, CSP frame-ancestors/object-src, Permissions-Policy.
- Sensitive data: CNIC, health / disability and counselling records are shown only to the person and the staff who handle them; backups never contain passwords or MFA secrets.
- Right of access: students download all their data (/api/student/my-data); the Institute Head exports or backs up the institute (/chairman/backups).
- Retention: records of students who left stay for the accreditation cycle (at least 6 years, the HEC/council audit window) and are then eligible for deletion on the institute's request; audit logs are kept 3 years.
- Backups: provider daily backups plus per-institute weekly backups (8 kept) with a safe restore of missing rows.
