# Getting started — first-week guide per role

The same steps appear in the app under **Getting Started** (/guide) and as the “Next step” tip at the top of each page.

## Chairman

As Institute Head you set up the institute once, name the people in charge, then watch and approve.

1. **Check institute name, logo and position titles** (`/chairman/institute-settings`) — Set the institute's name and logo, and the titles you use (VC, Rector, Director).
2. **Build the organisation chart** (`/chairman/org-chart`) — Add Deans, HODs, Program Leads; use “+ Add OMC & QEC Conveners”, then add members to each.
3. **Name the Controller of Examinations** (`/chairman/org-chart`) — Give the Controller of Examinations duty to one person; they run exams and publish results.
4. **Import earlier records** (`/data-import`) — Students, faculty, past transcripts and rooms — template or your own file, dry run first.
5. **Check academic rules and calendar** (`/academic-rules`) — CGPA limits and minimum attendance; then the academic calendar.
6. **Take the first backup** (`/chairman/backups`) — Download one now; weekly backups run on their own.
7. **Open My Approvals daily** (`/approvals`) — Everything waiting for you, in one list.

## Dean

As Dean you approve, watch your faculty's programs and lead accreditation visits.

1. **Look at your faculty** (`/dean/overview`) — Programs, teachers and results of your faculty at a glance.
2. **Clear My Approvals** (`/approvals`) — Leave, results, curricula and requests waiting for you.
3. **Check accreditation readiness** (`/accreditation/readiness`) — What each program still lacks for its council.
4. **Visit the Quality Office** (`/quality-office`) — New program approvals, IPE and reviews for your faculty.

## Head Of Department

As Head of Department you run your department's people and results.

1. **Open your department page** (`/hod/department`) — Teachers, programs and this semester's courses.
2. **Approve submitted results** (`/results-approval`) — Check grade distribution, then approve or return with a note.
3. **Handle leave requests** (`/leave-approvals`) — Approve leave and the class cover.
4. **See where your courses are used** (`/where-courses-used`) — Which programs depend on your department's teachers.

## Program Coordinator

As Program Lead you build the program and run each semester.

1. **Check your batches and GAs** (`/coordinator/batches`) — Each intake, its scheme of studies and graduate attributes.
2. **Assign Subject Experts** (`/coordinator/assign-subject-experts`) — One SE per course designs CLOs, lecture plan and assessment plan.
3. **Upload students** (`/data-import`) — Use Data Import (dry run first) or the batch upload.
4. **Plan the semester** (`/coordinator/semester`) — Offer courses, sections, dates; then the timetable.
5. **Look up a student's transcript** (`/coordinator/student-transcript`) — Type part of a roll number; use Previous / Next to go through a batch.

## Omc

As OMC member you review course designs and papers of your department.

1. **Open the review queue** (`/omc/queue`) — Course designs submitted by Subject Experts — approve or ask for changes.
2. **Vet question papers** (`/paper-vetting`) — Check papers against CLOs and Bloom levels before the exam.
3. **Check GA coverage** (`/ga-coverage`) — Every GA taught; labs mapped to Modern Tool Usage.

## Subject Expert

As Subject Expert you design the course once; every teacher of it uses your design.

1. **Open your courses** (`/subjectexpert/courses`) — Each course you design.
2. **Write CLOs and map them to GAs** (`/subjectexpert/courses`) — 3–5 CLOs with Bloom levels, each mapped to a GA.
3. **Lecture plan and assessment plan** (`/subjectexpert/courses`) — Topics per lecture mapped to CLOs; quizzes, assignments, mid and final.
4. **Submit to the OMC** (`/subjectexpert/courses`) — The OMC reviews and approves the design.

## Instructor

As teacher you take attendance, enter marks and submit results.

1. **Open your courses** (`/instructor/courses`) — Each section you teach this semester.
2. **Take attendance** (`/instructor/courses`) — Mark each class, or show the QR code for students to scan.
3. **Enter marks** (`/instructor/courses`) — Type marks, or import from Moodle / Google Classroom.
4. **Submit the result** (`/instructor/courses`) — Set grade cut-offs and submit; after approval the Controller publishes it.
5. **Complete your profile** (`/faculty/profile`) — Photo, qualifications, experience and publications (used for accreditation).

## Students

1. Sign in with your roll number; change the first password.
2. Check My Timetable and My Attendance.
3. Switch the portal to Urdu with the اردو button if you prefer.
4. Fees: print the challan or pay by bank / JazzCash / Easypaisa and enter the transaction number under My Fees.
5. Exams: print your admit card from My Exams; after results are published you may ask for re-checking.
